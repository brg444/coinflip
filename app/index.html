Predict: call where Bitcoin closes

Pick a line, a close a few minutes away, and whether BTC finishes above or below it. The desk takes the other side and locks your whole payout in a contract, and at the close the oracle’s signed price for that minute decides who gets it.

You win if BTC closes below your line. The further below today’s price you set it, the bigger the multiple.

A close exactly on the line goes to the desk. There are no partial payouts.

What the multiple means

The multiple is what you get back for every sat you stake if you are right. It comes from how likely the price is to finish on your side, read from Deribit’s Bitcoin options, less the desk’s 5% edge, so an even call pays 1.90×.

If settlement stalls

If a prediction cannot settle within 24 hours of the close, it voids and anyone can trigger a refund of your stake from the vault. If Arkade itself stays offline for more than 7 days, a separate backup recovery key (held independently of the desk) can spend an unsettled vault on Bitcoin L1 to issue that refund—you are trusting that backup key custodian to sign only valid refunds after 7 days.
