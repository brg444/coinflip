Locking BTC against a price move

Bitcoin’s price moves. Locking some of it until a date is useful: you get paid now for agreeing to a limit on that move.

Cap gains

You lock BTC until a date. You are paid now. If BTC stays under your line, you get the BTC back. If it goes above, you give up the gains above that line.

Take the loss

You lock BTC until a date. You are paid now. If BTC stays above your line, you get the BTC back. If it falls below, that loss comes out of what you locked.

Cap gains is selling a call. Take the loss is selling a put.

Why the put is different here

Settlement pays Bitcoin, not dollars. A cash-secured put sets dollars aside to pay the dollar drop. This only locks BTC, and that BTC is worth fewer dollars as the price falls, so it cannot promise an unlimited dollar loss. The instrument is a limited put: you cover the fall down to half your line. At half the line or lower, the desk keeps what you locked, and you keep only the payment you already received.
